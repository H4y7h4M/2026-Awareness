🛡️ Awareness Wallpapers Customization & Export Guide

A step-by-step visual walkthrough to customize the corporate logo and footer via Slide Master, and export high-definition wallpapers for employee screens.

Phase 1: Accessing Slide Master View

Open Awareness_Campaign_Bilingual_Wallpapers.pptx in Microsoft PowerPoint, then follow the two steps highlighted in the image below:

  • Step 1 In the top PowerPoint ribbon, click the View tab.
  • Step 2 In the Master Views group, click the Slide Master button.
2

Phase 2: Customizing Logo, Footer & Closing Master View

Once inside the Slide Master view, follow the exact numbering indicated on the screenshot:

  • Step 1 Select the Top Master Slide (#1): In the left thumbnail pane, scroll all the way to the top and select the primary Master Slide labeled "1".
    ⚠️ Important Note: Editing this top parent slide ensures all changes apply automatically across all campaign wallpapers at once.
  • Step 2 Replace or Delete Logo & Footer:
    • Company Logo: Click the placeholder box at the top left. Press Delete to remove it, or replace it with your company logo via Insert ⬅️ Pictures and position it in the same top-left area.
    • Footer Attribution: At the bottom right of the same slide, you can click the attribution box to delete it entirely or replace it with your department's name.
  • Step 3 Close Master View: When finished, click the red Close Master View (✕ Close) button in the ribbon to return to standard editing mode.
3

Phase 3: Exporting Wallpapers as High-Res Images

To extract the presentation slides as standalone high-resolution images (Full HD 1080p):

⌨️ Quick Shortcut: Press the F12 key on your keyboard in PowerPoint to directly open the Save As dialog window.
  • Step 03 In the Save as type dropdown menu, select:
    PNG Portable Network Graphics Format (*.png)
    Choose your target folder and click Save.
  • Step 04 A PowerPoint prompt will appear asking "Which slides do you want to export?"; click:
    All Slides.
🎉 All Done! PowerPoint will automatically generate a folder containing all 4 wallpapers in crisp 1920x1080 resolution, ready to deploy across company desktops or screensavers.
